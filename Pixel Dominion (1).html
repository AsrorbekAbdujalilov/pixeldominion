      Pixel Dominion

      Generate a world pixel by pixel, drop in the starting nations, and watch them expand, trade, ally, fight, rot from within and fall. Every country starts as a single pixel.

      Suggested for a normal gameEurope, 500 px, 30 countries, realistic unrest. Play at 2x and watch the Chronicle.

      Start from a real moment in European history. Borders are approximations built on the Europe map; from there the simulation takes over and history can diverge.
